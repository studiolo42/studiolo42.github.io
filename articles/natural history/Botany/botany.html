Introduction

Botany is one of the oldest sciences and one of the most consequential. Plants are the foundation of nearly every food chain on Earth, the primary producers of atmospheric oxygen, and the originators of most terrestrial ecosystems. To understand plants is to understand the architecture of life itself.

This collection follows Raven's Biology of Plants — the foundational university text — chapter by chapter, with detailed essays, visual schematics, flowcharts, and diagrams to make the material vivid and precise.
